# The Open Day Visit

- 天数：第 44 天
- 来源真题：Cambridge IELTS 1 Listening Test 1 / Section 3
- 来源文件：雅思真题/用于雅思学习skills数据/超给的资料/listening/cambridge-ielts-1/test1.md
- 听力片段：cambridge-ielts-1-test1-section3
- 复习内容：D43 当前生词 + 到期旧词 + 高负荷听力情节复习

## 复习生词

- dust - 灰尘
- burn - 燃烧；烧伤；烧坏
- achievement - 成就
- authentic - 真实的；正宗的
- remain - 保持；留下；仍然是
- expert - 专家
- count - 数数；计算；重要
- discovery - 发现
- step - 步骤；台阶；一步
- area - 区域；地区
- claim - 声称；要求；说法
- commemorate - 纪念

## 正文

1. Last Sunday, a new student visited the university open day.

   > 上周日，一名新生参观了大学开放日。
   >

2. She carried a small book in her bag for notes.

   > 她在包里带了一本小本子用来记笔记。
   >

3. She wanted to find the economics office before her first week began.

   > 她想在第一周开始前找到经济学办公室。
   >

4. She missed the welcome meeting on Saturday.

   > 她错过了周六的欢迎会。
   >

5. Because of that, she needed clear information from someone on campus.

   > 因此，她需要从校园里的人那里得到清楚的信息。
   >

6. At first, she followed a sign near the main gate.

   > 起初，她跟着正门附近的一个标志走。
   >

7. The sign led her to an old arts building.

   > 这个标志把她带到了一栋旧的艺术楼。
   >

8. Inside the building, she watched several students repair a notice board.

   > 在楼里，她看见几个学生修理一块公告板。
   >

9. One student counted the loose pins on the floor.

   > 一个学生数了数地上松掉的图钉。
   >

10. Another student cleaned the dust from a glass case.

   > 另一个学生擦掉了玻璃柜上的灰尘。
   >

11. She asked them about the economics office.

   > 她向他们询问经济学办公室的位置。
   >

12. The students pointed to the third floor.

   > 学生们指向三楼。
   >

13. After that, she climbed the wide stone steps.

   > 之后，她爬上了宽大的石头台阶。
   >

14. On the third floor, she found an area full of old books and maps.

   > 在三楼，她发现了一个摆满旧书和地图的区域。
   >

15. The room did not look like an office.

   > 这个房间看起来不像办公室。
   >

16. It looked like a small museum.

   > 它看起来像一个小博物馆。
   >

17. A sign near the door said the room commemorated the university’s first economics class.

   > 门边的一个标志写着，这个房间纪念这所大学的第一堂经济学课。
   >

18. She saw dust on a wooden desk near the window.

   > 她看见窗边的一张木桌上有灰尘。
   >

19. She also saw a dark burn mark on the edge of the desk.

   > 她还看见桌子边缘有一块深色的烧痕。
   >

20. A guide came into the room at ten o’clock.

   > 十点钟，一位讲解员走进了房间。
   >

21. He stood next to the desk and welcomed the visitors.

   > 他站在桌子旁边，欢迎参观者。
   >

22. He told them that a small fire burned part of the desk many years ago.

   > 他告诉他们，很多年前一场小火烧坏了这张桌子的一部分。
   >

23. He said the desk remained in the room because it was part of the school’s history.

   > 他说这张桌子留在房间里，因为它是学校历史的一部分。
   >

24. She looked at the burn mark again.

   > 她又看了看那块烧痕。
   >

25. She thought the mark made the desk feel more authentic.

   > 她觉得这块痕迹让这张桌子显得更加真实。
   >

26. The guide then showed an authentic letter from an early teacher.

   > 然后讲解员展示了一封早期教师写的真实信件。
   >

27. He claimed that the letter changed the way the course was taught.

   > 他声称这封信改变了这门课程的教学方式。
   >

28. She wrote the claim in her small book.

   > 她把这个说法写进了自己的小本子。
   >

29. After that, she asked the guide about the real economics office.

   > 之后，她向讲解员询问真正的经济学办公室在哪里。
   >

30. The guide smiled and told her that the office moved last year.

   > 讲解员笑了笑，并告诉她办公室去年搬走了。
   >

31. He walked with her to a more modern building across the yard.

   > 他陪她走到院子对面一栋更现代的楼。
   >

32. On the way, he counted three wrong signs near the path.

   > 路上，他数了数小路附近的三个错误标志。
   >

33. He said those signs caused trouble for many new students.

   > 他说那些标志给许多新生带来了麻烦。
   >

34. At the modern building, a teacher came out of a small office.

   > 在那栋现代楼里，一位老师从一间小办公室里走了出来。
   >

35. The guide introduced the student to the teacher.

   > 讲解员把这名学生介绍给了那位老师。
   >

36. The teacher was an expert in first-year economics.

   > 那位老师是大一经济学方面的专家。
   >

37. She explained that she missed the meeting.

   > 她解释说自己错过了会议。
   >

38. The expert gave her a printed sheet.

   > 那位专家给了她一张打印的资料单。
   >

39. She read the first step on the sheet.

   > 她读了资料单上的第一步。
   >

40. The first step told her to go to two classes each week.

   > 第一步告诉她每周要去上两节课。
   >

41. The teacher said being there counted a lot in the course.

   > 老师说出席这件事在这门课中很重要。
   >

42. She counted the class times with her finger.

   > 她用手指算了算上课时间。
   >

43. She noticed that one class began before her part-time job.

   > 她注意到有一节课在她兼职工作前开始。
   >

44. She worried because the class ended too late.

   > 她很担心，因为那节课结束得太晚。
   >

45. The teacher told her that the rule remained strict.

   > 老师告诉她，这条规定仍然很严格。
   >

46. She then asked about the small group class.

   > 然后她询问小组课的情况。
   >

47. The teacher offered three times during the week.

   > 老师提供了一周内的三个时间。
   >

48. She chose the Monday class.

   > 她选择了周一的课。
   >

49. After that, the teacher described the course work.

   > 之后，老师介绍了课程作业。
   >

50. He told her that each student gave a short talk.

   > 他告诉她每个学生都要做一次短报告。
   >

51. He also told her that each student wrote a long essay.

   > 他还告诉她每个学生都要写一篇长论文。
   >

52. She felt nervous, but she wrote each task down.

   > 她感到紧张，但她把每项任务都写了下来。
   >

53. The teacher said students could use books in the room during the exam.

   > 老师说学生们考试时可以使用房间里的书。
   >

54. She thought this rule sounded easy at first.

   > 她起初觉得这条规则听起来很容易。
   >

55. Then the teacher explained that good results still needed hard work.

   > 然后老师解释说，好成绩仍然需要努力。
   >

56. After that, he took her to the library area.

   > 之后，他带她去了图书馆区域。
   >

57. In that area, important books remained on a special shelf.

   > 在那个区域，重要书籍留在一个特殊书架上。
   >

58. Students read the books there, but they did not take them home.

   > 学生们在那里阅读这些书，但不能把它们带回家。
   >

59. She made a useful discovery near the shelf.

   > 她在书架附近有了一个有用的发现。
   >

60. She found an old prize list with her grandfather’s name on it.

   > 她发现了一张旧获奖名单，上面有她祖父的名字。
   >

61. The teacher said her grandfather’s study was a real achievement.

   > 老师说她祖父的学习成绩是一项真正的成就。
   >

62. She felt proud of that achievement.

   > 她为那项成就感到自豪。
   >

63. Finally, she thanked the teacher and asked a friend to take her work time.

   > 最后，她感谢了老师，并请一位朋友替她上班。
   >

64. That evening, she came home with clear notes and a better plan.

   > 那天晚上，她带着清楚的笔记和更好的计划回到了家。
   >

65. Last Sunday, the visit changed her fear into a calm start.

   > 上周日，那次参观把她的害怕变成了平静的开始。
   >
