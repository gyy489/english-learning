# An Ordinary Day in the Garden

- 天数：第 33 天
- 来源真题：无（纯生词复习）
- 来源文件：无（纯生词复习）

## 正文

1. It was an ordinary day in the garden.

   > 花园里是普通的一天。
   >

2. I began my day with a simple breakfast, using a spoon to stir my coffee.

   > 我用勺子搅拌咖啡，开始了简单的早餐。
   >

3. After breakfast, I took a step into the garden, ready to enjoy the fresh air.

   > 早餐后，我迈入花园，准备享受新鲜空气。
   >

4. The flowers were blooming, and everything felt smooth and calm.

   > 花儿盛开，一切都感觉光滑而宁静。
   >

5. Suddenly, I made an interesting discovery under the old tree.

   > 突然，我在那棵老树下有了一个有趣的发现。
   >

6. There was a small, uneven patch of land that needed attention.

   > 有一小块不平坦的土地需要注意。
   >

7. I decided to fix it, thinking it could be harmful to the plants.

   > 我决定修理它，觉得对植物有害。
   >

8. The first step was to remove the weeds around it.

   > 第一步是清除周围的杂草。
   >

9. As I worked, I felt a sense of peace that was almost religious.

   > 工作时，我感到一种几乎宗教般的宁静。
   >

10. My neighbor came over to help and we started to argue about the best method.

    > 我的邻居过来帮忙，我们开始争论最佳方法。
    >

11. After a short discussion, we agreed to smooth out the soil together.

    > 简短讨论后，我们同意一起将土壤弄平。
    >

12. We used our tools carefully, making sure not to frustrate each other's efforts.

    > 我们小心地使用工具，确保不挫败彼此的努力。
    >

13. By lunchtime, we had built a small fence around the garden.

    > 到午餐时间，我们在花园周围建了一个小栅栏。
    >

14. It was a tiring job, but the sense of achievement was worth it.

    > 这是个累人的工作，但成就感是值得的。
    >

15. We took a break and recorded our progress with photos.

    > 我们休息了一下，并拍照片记录了我们的进展。
    >

16. As we rested, the sunlight filtered through the leaves, casting an uneven pattern.

    > 我们休息时，阳光穿过树叶，投下不均匀的图案。
    >

17. We enjoyed this simple beauty and shared stories of previous gardening experiences.

    > 我们欣赏这种简单的美，并分享过去的园艺经验。
    >

18. It felt relaxing, a welcome escape from the usual day-to-day concerns.

    > 这感觉很放松，是从日常烦恼中解脱的欢迎之举。
    >

19. In the afternoon, we continued to work smoothly, with the garden gradually taking shape.

    > 下午，我们顺利地继续工作，花园逐渐成型。
    >

20. The tasks might have seemed ordinary, but they brought us joy and satisfaction.

    > 任务看似普通，却带给我们快乐和满足。
    >

21. As the sun began to set, we felt a sense of accomplishment.

    > 太阳开始下山时，我们感到一种成就感。
    >

22. The garden, once uneven and unkempt, now looked tidy and inviting.

    > 曾经不平整和杂乱的花园，现在看起来整洁而诱人。
    >

23. My neighbor and I shook hands, thanking each other for the teamwork.

    > 我和邻居握手，感谢彼此的团队合作。
    >

24. We promised to continue improving the garden, step by step.

    > 我们承诺一步一步继续改善花园。
    >

25. Gardening, we agreed, was a peaceful yet rewarding activity.

    > 我们同意，园艺是一项平静但有收获的活动。
    >

26. As I entered my house, I couldn't help but smile at the day's work.

    > 走进房子时，我不禁为一天的工作微笑。
    >

27. The garden wasn't just a place for plants; it was a place for friendship and growth.

    > 花园不仅是植物的地方，也是友谊和成长的地方。
    >

28. With everything done, I relaxed, content with the day's peaceful achievements.

    > 一切都做完后，我放松了，为一天宁静的成就感到满足。
    >
