# A Museum Day in Autumn

- 天数：第 26 天
- 来源真题：无（纯生词复习）
- 来源文件：无（纯生词复习）
- 复习内容：D25 当前生词 + 到期旧词 + 纯单词模式（积压清理）

## 复习生词

- frustrate - 使沮丧
- heavy - 重的；沉重的
- slide - 滑动；滑梯
- during - 在...期间
- across - 穿过；在对面
- behind - 在后面
- ceremony - 仪式
- common - 常见的；普通的
- debate - 辩论
- fall - 落下；秋天
- gently - 轻柔地
- journey - 旅程
- leaf - 树叶
- museum - 博物馆
- practise - 练习

## 正文

1. Autumn is a common season for school trips in our city.

   > 秋天是我们城市学校郊游的常见季节。
   >

2. During this week, my class plans a journey to the city museum.

   > 这周，我们班计划了一次去市博物馆的旅行。
   >

3. We will walk across the old town to reach the museum gate.

   > 我们将穿过老城区到达博物馆大门。
   >

4. Before we leave, our teacher gives a short opening ceremony in the classroom.

   > 出发前，老师在教室里举行了一个简短的开幕仪式。
   >

5. She reminds us to practise walking in a quiet line.

   > 她提醒我们练习安静地排队走路。
   >

6. My backpack feels heavy because I bring my notebook and a water bottle.

   > 我的背包感觉很重，因为我带了笔记本和水瓶。
   >

7. Some classmates walk behind the group and look at the falling leaves.

   > 一些同学走在队伍后面，看着落下的树叶。
   >

8. The wind gently pushes yellow leaves across the street.

   > 风轻轻地把黄色的树叶吹过街道。
   >

9. During our walk, two students start a small debate about the fastest way to the museum.

   > 在我们走路的时候，两名学生就去博物馆最快的路开始了一场小辩论。
   >

10. One student says a shortcut behind the old bridge is faster.

   > 一名学生说老桥后面的一条近路更快。
   >

11. The other student does not agree, and this makes her a little frustrated.

   > 另一名学生不同意，这让她有点沮丧。
   >

12. Our teacher listens to the debate and gently helps them find a fair answer.

   > 老师听了这场辩论，温和地帮助他们找到一个公平的答案。
   >

13. We finally cross the street and see the museum in front of us.

   > 我们终于穿过街道，看到博物馆就在我们面前。
   >

14. Inside the museum, a small ceremony welcomes new visitors every morning.

   > 在博物馆里，每天早上都有一个小仪式欢迎新的参观者。
   >

15. We stand behind a rope and watch the short welcome ceremony.

   > 我们站在绳子后面，观看简短的欢迎仪式。
   >

16. During the tour, a guide tells us about a common tool from ancient times.

   > 在参观过程中，导游给我们讲了一件古代常见的工具。
   >

17. Some students practise taking notes about the tools they see.

   > 一些学生练习记录他们看到的工具的笔记。
   >

18. My friend Tom looks tired because his bag feels too heavy today.

   > 我的朋友汤姆看起来很累，因为他今天的包太重了。
   >

19. He puts his heavy bag down and rests for a while.

   > 他把沉重的书包放下，休息了一会儿。
   >

20. Near the exit, there is a long slide for children to play on.

   > 在出口附近，有一个长长的滑梯供孩子们玩耍。
   >

21. A few younger kids laugh and slide down happily.

   > 几个年幼的孩子笑着开心地滑下来。
   >

22. Watching them slide makes our whole class smile.

   > 看着他们滑下来让我们全班都笑了。
   >

23. This museum journey is not a common school day for us.

   > 这次博物馆之旅对我们来说不是普通的一天。
   >

24. We learn that a journey can teach more than a classroom lesson.

   > 我们了解到一次旅行能教给我们比课堂更多的东西。
   >

25. On the way back, dry leaves fall gently onto the road.

   > 在回去的路上，干燥的树叶轻轻地落在路上。
   >

26. I feel a little frustrated that the day passes so quickly.

   > 我有点沮丧，这一天过得这么快。
   >

27. Still, this journey behind us will stay in my memory for a long time.

   > 尽管如此，这次已经过去的旅行会长久留在我的记忆中。
   >
