# A Special Day at the Museum

- 天数：第 43 天
- 来源真题：Cambridge IELTS 1 Listening Test 3 / Section 2
- 来源文件：雅思真题/用于雅思学习skills数据/超给的资料/listening/cambridge-ielts-1/test3.md
- 听力片段：cambridge-ielts-1-test3-section2
- 复习内容：D42 当前生词 + 到期旧词 + 高负荷听力情节复习 + 一般过去时专项

## 复习生词

- crowd - 人群
- gather - 聚集；收集
- auction - 拍卖
- dust - 灰尘
- waste - 浪费
- roof - 屋顶
- burn - 燃烧；烧伤
- achievement - 成就
- pot - 锅；罐
- authentic - 真实的；正宗的
- authorship - 作者身份；著作权

## 正文

1. Last Saturday, a student visited a sea museum with two friends.

   > 上周六，米娅和两名同学参观了一座海洋博物馆。
   >

2. The museum stood near a busy port.

   > 博物馆坐落在一个繁忙的港口旁边。
   >

3. Several old ships formed part of the museum.

   > 几艘老船构成了博物馆的一部分。
   >

4. A large crowd waited inside the front hall.

   > 一大群人在前厅里等候。
   >

5. The visitors gathered around their guide.

   > 游客们聚集在一位名叫海伦的导游周围。
   >

6. The guide welcomed everyone and explained the plan.

   > 海伦欢迎了大家，并说明了参观计划。
   >

7. She said the tour lasted about one hour.

   > 她说参观大约持续一个小时。
   >

8. The information desk was their final meeting place.

   > 服务台是他们最后集合的地点。
   >

9. Green signs showed the way to the education centre.

   > 绿色箭头标出了通往教育中心的路。
   >

10. A small library occupied the top floor.

   > 一座小图书馆位于顶层。
   >

11. The student and her friends followed the signs to the top floor.

   > 米娅和同学们沿着箭头上了楼。
   >

12. The library windows looked across the water.

   > 图书馆的窗户面向水面。
   >

13. An old model ship sat under a glass cover.

   > 一艘古老的模型船放在玻璃罩下面。
   >

14. A thin layer of dust covered its wooden roof.

   > 一层薄薄的灰尘盖在它的木制船顶上。
   >

15. One student wanted to clean it, but a museum worker stopped him.

   > 一名学生想清理它，但工作人员阻止了他。
   >

16. The worker said poor cleaning damaged old objects.

   > 工作人员说，粗心的清洁会损坏老物件。
   >

17. His warning saved the student from making a mistake.

   > 他的提醒避免了那名学生犯错。
   >

18. The group then entered a room about long sea journeys.

   > 随后，一行人进入了一个介绍漫长海上旅程的展厅。
   >

19. Old bags and dishes filled the room.

   > 旧箱包和餐具摆满了房间。
   >

20. A black cooking pot stood near a small bed.

   > 一口黑色的锅放在一张小床附近。
   >

21. The pot came from a ship that crossed the sea many times.

   > 这口锅来自一艘多次横渡大海的船。
   >

22. Its marks showed that cooks had used it over a fire.

   > 锅上的痕迹表明，厨师们曾把它放在火上使用。
   >

23. One side had burned, but the pot remained strong.

   > 锅的一侧被烧过，但它仍然很结实。
   >

24. The student thought the simple object told an authentic story.

   > 米娅认为，这件简单的物品讲述了一个真实的故事。
   >

25. Her friends agreed with her.

   > 她的同学们同意她的看法。
   >

26. Each object carried a different part of the past.

   > 每件物品都承载着过去不同的一部分。
   >

27. The next room displayed pictures by Australian artists.

   > 下一个展厅展出了澳大利亚艺术家的画作。
   >

28. One picture showed a group repairing a ship.

   > 一幅画描绘了水手们修理船只的情景。
   >

29. Another picture showed families waiting by the sea.

   > 另一幅画描绘了在海边等待的家庭。
   >

30. The names below the pictures identified most of the artists.

   > 画作下方的名字标明了大多数艺术家的身份。
   >

31. However, one picture had no clear authorship.

   > 然而，其中一幅画的作者身份并不明确。
   >

32. Two experts studied it, but they reached different answers.

   > 两名专家研究了它，但得出了不同的答案。
   >

33. The student enjoyed the mystery and added it to her notes.

   > 米娅很喜欢这个谜团，并把它记在了笔记本里。
   >

34. After that, the guide led everyone outside.

   > 之后，导游带领大家走到室外。
   >

35. An old ship waited near the museum.

   > 一艘老船停靠在博物馆旁边。
   >

36. Its narrow steps caused a problem for some visitors.

   > 狭窄的楼梯给一些游客带来了困难。
   >

37. The guide asked those visitors to remain on the ground.

   > 海伦请那些游客留在地面上。
   >

38. The others climbed slowly and reached the open deck.

   > 其他人慢慢爬上去，到达了露天甲板。
   >

39. Strong wind moved across the deck.

   > 强风吹过甲板。
   >

40. The ship's high roof protected part of the group.

   > 船上的高顶棚为一部分人挡住了风。
   >

41. The student imagined how the ship workers had lived there years before.

   > 米娅想象着多年前水手们如何在那里生活。
   >

42. Their journeys were a great human achievement.

   > 他们的航行是一项伟大的人类成就。
   >

43. The visit ended with a small auction in the main hall.

   > 这次参观以主厅里的一场小型拍卖结束。
   >

44. The museum sold copies of several popular pictures.

   > 博物馆出售了几幅受欢迎画作的复制品。
   >

45. The money supported the care of the old ships.

   > 这些钱用于支持老船的维护。
   >

46. A local teacher bought the first picture.

   > 一位当地教师买下了第一幅画。
   >

47. Two families offered money for the second picture.

   > 两个家庭为第二幅画出价。
   >

48. The auction became busy, and the crowd made more noise.

   > 拍卖变得热闹起来，人群的声音也更大了。
   >

49. The student did not buy anything because she wanted to save money.

   > 米娅没有买任何东西，因为她想省钱。
   >

50. She believed a rushed choice could waste both money and space.

   > 她认为，仓促的选择可能会浪费金钱和空间。
   >

51. Her friends bought one card each from the museum shop.

   > 她的同学们每人从博物馆商店买了一张卡片。
   >

52. Everyone returned to the information desk on time.

   > 所有人都按时回到了服务台。
   >

53. The guide counted the visitors and found that no one was missing.

   > 海伦清点了游客，发现没有人掉队。
   >

54. The three students thanked her for the useful tour.

   > 三名学生感谢她带来的这次有益参观。
   >

55. They left the museum with new knowledge and happy memories.

   > 他们带着新知识和愉快的回忆离开了博物馆。
   >
