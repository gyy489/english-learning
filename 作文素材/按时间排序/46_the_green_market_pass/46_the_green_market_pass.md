# The Green Market Pass

- 天数：第 46 天

## 复习生词

- authentic - 真实的；可信的
- uncertainty - 不确定；疑惑
- trade - 交换；交易
- pressure - 压力
- thin - 薄的；细的
- describe - 描述
- sound - 听起来；声音
- although - 尽管；虽然
- rope - 绳子
- crowd - 人群
- gather - 聚集；收集
- glad - 高兴的
- sale - 出售；特卖活动
- auction - 拍卖
- authorship - 作者身份；著作权归属

## 正文

1. Emma wanted a stand at the university’s first Green Market.

   > 艾玛想在学校第一届绿色集市上要一个摊位。
   >

2. Her plan was to help students trade used cups, lunch boxes, and shopping bags.

   > 她计划帮助学生交换用过的杯子、饭盒和购物袋。
   >

3. She also wanted to show the waste from single-use packaging.

   > 她还想展示一次性包装产生的垃圾。
   >

4. However, she did not know whether students needed a special pass for a stand.

   > 然而，她不知道学生开摊位是否需要特别通行证。
   >

5. This uncertainty stopped her from printing information about her stand.

   > 这种不确定让她无法开始印制摊位的相关信息。
   >

6. She asked Leo, who had worked at a campus event before.

   > 她询问了利奥，因为他以前参加过一次校园活动。
   >

7. Leo said that the Green Office could describe the rules clearly.

   > 利奥说，绿色办公室可以清楚地说明规定。
   >

8. “I think only university groups can apply,” he added.

   > “我想只有校内团体才能申请，”他补充道。
   >

9. That did not sound fair to Emma because many local people wanted to help.

   > 艾玛觉得这听起来不公平，因为许多当地居民也想帮忙。
   >

10. Leo explained that space in the main square was limited.

   > 利奥解释说，主广场的空间有限。
   >

11. Too large a crowd could block the path to the library.

   > 过于庞大的人群可能会堵住通往图书馆的道路。
   >

12. The market staff also faced pressure to keep it clean and safe.

   > 集市工作人员还面临着保持场地清洁和安全的压力。
   >

13. Emma asked where she could find the Green Office.

   > 艾玛询问在哪里能找到绿色办公室。
   >

14. Leo told her it was next to the sports hall, across from the bike park.

   > 利奥告诉她，办公室在体育馆旁边、单车停车区对面。
   >

15. He told her not to enter the old office near the lake.

   > 他提醒她不要走进湖边的旧办公室。
   >

16. Although its sign was still there, the building had been closed for months.

   > 虽然那里的标牌还在，但那栋楼已经关闭好几个月了。
   >

17. Emma followed a row of green signs and soon found the correct building.

   > 艾玛沿着一排绿色标牌，很快找到了正确的大楼。
   >

18. At the desk, a staff member asked about her project.

   > 在服务台，一名工作人员询问了她的项目。
   >

19. Emma said that visitors would bring clean items and trade them instead of buying new ones.

   > 艾玛说，来访者会带来干净的物品进行交换，而不是购买新品。
   >

20. The staff member was glad to hear that the project helped people use things again.

   > 工作人员很高兴听到这个项目能帮助人们再次使用旧物品。
   >

21. She said the market had too many food stands and not enough projects like Emma’s.

   > 她说，集市上的食品摊位太多，而像艾玛这样的项目却不够。
   >

22. Still, Emma had to complete a short application.

   > 尽管如此，艾玛仍然必须填写一份简短的申请表。
   >

23. The staff member needed her name, group address, and student number.

   > 工作人员需要她的姓名、团体地址和学号。
   >

24. She also asked how many people would work at the stand.

   > 她还询问会有多少人在摊位工作。
   >

25. Emma wrote that six people would help, but the number was hard to read.

   > 艾玛写下会有六个人帮忙，但那个数字很难看清。
   >

26. The uncertainty over the exact number caused a short delay.

   > 对确切人数的不确定造成了短暂的延误。
   >

27. After checking her messages, Emma confirmed the number and wrote it again.

   > 查看消息后，艾玛确认了人数，并重新写了一遍。
   >

28. The staff member then explained that every van needed a green pass in its front window.

   > 工作人员接着解释说，每辆货车的前窗都需要放置一张绿色通行证。
   >

29. Without the pass, the team could not bring a van into the market area.

   > 如果没有这张通行证，团队就不能把货车开进集市区域。
   >

30. Emma had planned to carry several heavy boxes by van.

   > 艾玛原本计划用货车运送几个沉重的箱子。
   >

31. The staff member told her to use strong boxes that could be used again rather than thin plastic ones.

   > 工作人员让她使用能再次利用的结实箱子，而不是薄塑料箱。
   >

32. Thin boxes often broke when people filled them with glass bottles.

   > 薄箱子装满玻璃瓶后经常会破裂。
   >

33. Emma agreed and asked where she could pay for the pass.

   > 艾玛同意了，并询问在哪里可以支付通行证费用。
   >

34. The payment desk was at the end of the hall.

   > 缴费处位于走廊尽头。
   >

35. Unfortunately, it had closed ten minutes earlier for lunch.

   > 不巧的是，那里十分钟前已经关门午休了。
   >

36. It would not open again until two o’clock.

   > 它要到两点才会重新开放。
   >

37. Emma felt pressure because she had to send the final details of her stand that afternoon.

   > 艾玛感到压力，因为她必须在当天下午提交摊位的最终信息。
   >

38. The staff member suggested that she use the waiting time to look around the market area.

   > 工作人员建议她利用等待时间去看看集市场地。
   >

39. Outside, Emma found volunteers trying to hang a large sign.

   > 在外面，艾玛发现志愿者们正试图挂起一块大标牌。
   >

40. Their rope was too thin to hold it in the wind.

   > 他们的绳子太细，无法在风中固定住标牌。
   >

41. Emma offered them a stronger rope from her supply box.

   > 艾玛把物资箱里一根更结实的绳子提供给了他们。
   >

42. In return, they helped her measure the space for her stand.

   > 作为回报，他们帮她测量了摊位空间。
   >

43. One volunteer said that the market would also include a sale of repaired furniture.

   > 一名志愿者说，集市还会出售修复过的家具。
   >

44. The best items would be offered in a small auction.

   > 最好的物品会在一场小型拍卖中出售。
   >

45. The money would pay for more public water stations around the campus.

   > 这些钱将用于在校园各处增设公共饮水站。
   >

46. Those new stations would help people avoid single-use water bottles.

   > 那些新饮水站将帮助人们避免使用一次性水瓶。
   >

47. Emma offered to gather stories from people who had repaired the furniture.

   > 艾玛主动提出收集那些修复家具的人所讲的故事。
   >

48. She wanted each card to tell an authentic story about an old item.

   > 她希望每张说明卡都讲述一个旧物品的真实故事。
   >

49. The market staff agreed, but they asked her to record the authorship of every story.

   > 集市工作人员同意了，但要求她记录每个故事的作者身份。
   >

50. This would name each writer and prevent false claims.

   > 这样可以标明每位作者，并防止虚假说法。
   >

51. Emma began to see that the market was more than a place for trade.

   > 艾玛开始意识到，这个集市不仅是一个交易场所。
   >

52. It connected waste reduction with honest community action.

   > 它把减少垃圾与真诚的社区行动联系了起来。
   >

53. At two o’clock, she returned to the payment desk.

   > 两点钟时，她回到了缴费处。
   >

54. The worker accepted her form but noticed that no group name was listed.

   > 工作人员接收了她的表格，但发现上面没有填写团体名称。
   >

55. Emma quickly chose the name “Use It Again.”

   > 艾玛很快选定了“再次使用”这个名字。
   >

56. She received the pass and placed it in the front window of the van.

   > 她拿到了通行证，并把它放在货车的前窗上。
   >

57. On market day, students and local families began to gather around her stand.

   > 集市当天，学生和当地家庭开始聚集在她的摊位周围。
   >

58. By twelve o’clock, they had exchanged more than one hundred used items.

   > 到十二点时，他们已经交换了一百多件旧物品。
   >

59. The sale and auction raised enough money for the first new water station.

   > 特卖活动和拍卖筹到了足够的钱，可以增设第一个新饮水站。
   >

60. Emma was glad that one small question about a pass had led to authentic change across the community.

   > 艾玛很高兴，一个关于通行证的小问题最终给整个社区带来了真正的改变。
   >
