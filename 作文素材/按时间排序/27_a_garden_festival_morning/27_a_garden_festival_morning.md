# A Garden Festival Morning

- 天数：第 27 天
- 来源真题：无（纯生词复习）
- 来源文件：无（纯生词复习）
- 复习内容：D26 当前生词 + 到期旧词 + 纯单词模式（积压清理）

## 复习生词

- heavy - 重的；沉重的
- slide - 滑动
- across - 穿过；遍及
- debate - 争论；讨论
- journey - 旅程；过程
- prepare - 准备
- religious - 宗教的
- remind - 提醒
- shake - 摇晃；发抖
- warm - 温暖的
- awake - 醒着的；清醒的
- garden - 花园
- expect - 期待；预料
- modern - 现代的
- shape - 形状；塑造

## 正文

1. I wake up early, and I am fully awake before six o'clock.

   > 我很早醒来，六点前就完全清醒了。
   >

2. Today our neighborhood will hold a small festival in the community garden.

   > 今天我们社区将在花园里举办一个小型的节日活动。
   >

3. I expect many neighbors to come and enjoy the morning.

   > 我期待很多邻居来享受这个早晨。
   >

4. The weather feels warm, so people can sit outside comfortably.

   > 天气感觉很温暖，所以人们可以舒适地坐在外面。
   >

5. I prepare some chairs and tables before the guests arrive.

   > 我在客人到来之前准备了一些椅子和桌子。
   >

6. My father reminds me to bring water for the plants.

   > 我父亲提醒我给植物带水。
   >

7. Some flower pots are heavy, so we carry them together.

   > 一些花盆很重，所以我们一起搬。
   >

8. We walk across the garden to find a good spot for the tables.

   > 我们穿过花园找一个放桌子的好地方。
   >

9. A short slide stands near the trees for the children.

   > 一个小滑梯立在树旁边，供孩子们玩耍。
   >

10. Kids laugh and slide down again and again.

   > 孩子们笑着一次又一次地滑下来。
   >

11. My grandmother tells us about a religious custom from her hometown.

   > 我奶奶给我们讲了她家乡的一个宗教习俗。
   >

12. She says the garden's shape is like an old symbol from that custom.

   > 她说花园的形状像那个习俗里的一个古老符号。
   >

13. My cousin disagrees and starts a friendly debate about the garden's design.

   > 我表姐不同意，就花园的设计开始了一场友好的辩论。
   >

14. He thinks a modern shape would look better than the old one.

   > 他认为现代的形状看起来会更好。
   >

15. Their debate makes everyone smile instead of feeling upset.

   > 他们的辩论让每个人都笑了，而不是感到不高兴。
   >

16. Suddenly, wind begins to shake the branches above us.

   > 突然，风开始摇动我们头顶上的树枝。
   >

17. I hold my heavy bag close so nothing falls out.

   > 我把沉重的包抱紧，这样东西就不会掉出来。
   >

18. After a short journey around the garden, we return to the tables.

   > 绕花园走了一小段旅程后，我们回到了桌子旁。
   >

19. More neighbors arrive, and the garden feels warm and lively.

   > 更多邻居到了，花园感觉温暖而热闹。
   >

20. We prepare tea and simple snacks for everyone.

   > 我们为大家准备了茶和简单的小吃。
   >

21. A young boy is still not fully awake, so he sits quietly.

   > 一个小男孩还没完全清醒，所以他安静地坐着。
   >

22. My father reminds everyone that the festival is about kindness, not competition.

   > 我父亲提醒大家，这个节日是关于善意，而不是竞争。
   >

23. The old religious story behind the festival makes people feel closer.

   > 这个节日背后古老的宗教故事让人们感觉更亲近。
   >

24. We walk across the garden one more time before saying goodbye.

   > 在告别之前，我们再一次穿过花园。
   >

25. This short journey through the garden already feels like a warm, heavy memory to me.

   > 对我来说，这次穿过花园的短暂旅程已经像一段温暖而沉甸甸的回忆。
   >

26. I feel glad that our small debate about the garden ends in laughter.

   > 我很高兴我们关于花园的小小争论以笑声结束。
   >
