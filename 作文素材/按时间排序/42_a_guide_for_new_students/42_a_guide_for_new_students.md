# A Guide for New Students

- 天数：第 42 天
- 来源真题：Cambridge IELTS 1 Listening Test 2 / Section 1
- 来源文件：雅思真题/用于雅思学习skills数据/超给的资料/listening/cambridge-ielts-1/test2.md
- 听力片段：cambridge-ielts-1-test2-section1
- 复习内容：D41 当前生词 + 到期旧词 + 高负荷听力情节复习

## 复习生词

- empty - 空的
- engage - 参与；吸引；使投入
- front - 前面；正面
- messy - 凌乱的
- shelf - 架子
- crowd - 人群
- seller - 卖家
- gather - 收集；聚集
- engag - engage 的不完整拼写；参与
- although - 虽然；尽管
- auction - 拍卖

## 正文

1. Mary worked at the student help office in Cambridge.

   > 玛丽在剑桥的学生帮助办公室工作。
   >

2. Her job that week was to gather advice for a guide for new overseas students.

   > 那一周，她的工作是为新来的海外学生指南收集建议。
   >

3. She did not want the guide to sound like a cold list of rules.

   > 她不想让这本指南听起来像一份冷冰冰的规则清单。
   >

4. She wanted real stories from students who had already lived through the first hard months.

   > 她想要来自已经经历过最初艰难几个月的学生的真实故事。
   >

5. So she invited two students, Kate and Luke, to talk with her after lunch.

   > 所以她邀请了两名学生凯特和卢克在午饭后和她谈谈。
   >

6. The small meeting room was almost empty when Kate arrived.

   > 凯特到达时，小会议室几乎是空的。
   >

7. Only three chairs stood near the front window.

   > 只有三把椅子放在前窗附近。
   >

8. A shelf covered with dust stood against the wall.

   > 一个落了灰的架子靠墙放着。
   >

9. Kate smiled, although she looked tired.

   > 凯特笑了笑，尽管她看起来很累。
   >

10. Mary thanked her for coming and said the talk would help people who had not arrived yet.

   > 玛丽感谢她前来，并说这次谈话会帮助那些还没到的学生。
   >

11. Kate said her first evening in the town had felt strange.

   > 凯特说，她到这个城镇的第一个晚上感觉很奇怪。
   >

12. The streets were clean, but they were so quiet that they seemed empty.

   > 街道很干净，但安静得像是空无一人。
   >

13. In her home city, a crowd was still outside after dark.

   > 在她的家乡城市，天黑后外面仍然有人群。
   >

14. In Cambridge, she heard only soft steps and a church bell.

   > 在剑桥，她只听到轻轻的脚步声和教堂钟声。
   >

15. Mary wrote this down because quiet evenings could surprise new students.

   > 玛丽把这一点记了下来，因为安静的夜晚可能会让新学生感到意外。
   >

16. Then she asked Kate about her first place to live.

   > 然后她问凯特最初住的地方。
   >

17. Kate said she had stayed in a student building near the college.

   > 凯特说她曾住在学院附近的一栋学生楼里。
   >

18. At first, it seemed useful because meals were included.

   > 起初，那似乎很方便，因为包含了餐食。
   >

19. But the food was poor, and the room for meals often felt cold.

   > 但食物很差，而且吃饭的房间常常让人觉得冷清。
   >

20. Her own room had one bed, one desk, and one narrow shelf.

   > 她自己的房间里有一张床、一张桌子和一个窄架子。
   >

21. The shelf was soon full, but the room still felt lonely.

   > 架子很快就满了，但房间仍然让人觉得孤单。
   >

22. Mary asked whether the other students had helped her feel at home.

   > 玛丽问其他学生是否帮助她有了家的感觉。
   >

23. Kate said no and looked at the messy notes in her bag.

   > 凯特说没有，并看了看包里凌乱的笔记。
   >

24. She said the building was full, but people did not really engage with each other.

   > 她说那栋楼住满了人，但人们并没有真正互相交流。
   >

25. They passed in the hall, said hello, and then shut their doors.

   > 他们在走廊里擦肩而过，说声你好，然后就关上门。
   >

26. A crowd lived there, but it did not feel like a group.

   > 那里住着一大群人，但感觉并不像一个集体。
   >

27. Mary understood why this was a serious problem.

   > 玛丽明白为什么这是个严重的问题。
   >

28. A student could have a room and meals, but still have no one to talk to.

   > 一个学生可能有房间和饭食，但仍然没有人可以交谈。
   >

29. Kate then spoke about her course.

   > 然后凯特谈到了她的课程。
   >

30. She liked the subject and enjoyed much of the reading.

   > 她喜欢这个学科，也喜欢其中很多阅读内容。
   >

31. Although the classes were interesting, she rarely had time to speak with the teachers.

   > 尽管课程很有趣，她却很少有时间和老师交谈。
   >

32. The teachers were kind when she met them, but they were always busy.

   > 她见到老师时，他们都很友好，但他们总是很忙。
   >

33. On a field trip, she finally had a good talk with one teacher.

   > 在一次实地学习中，她终于和一位老师好好谈了一次。
   >

34. After that, she wished there were regular meetings every two weeks.

   > 在那之后，她希望每两周能有固定的会面。
   >

35. Mary wrote, “help students engage with teachers,” at the top of her page.

   > 玛丽在纸的上方写下：“帮助学生和老师交流。”
   >

36. One note on her paper even said “engag” because she had written engage too fast.

   > 她纸上的一条笔记甚至写成了“engag”，因为她把 engage 写得太快了。
   >

37. Mary smiled and fixed the word before Luke came in.

   > 玛丽笑了笑，并在卢克进来前改好了这个词。
   >

38. Luke arrived with a wet jacket and a calm face.

   > 卢克穿着湿外套来了，表情很平静。
   >

39. He said he was from Indonesia and that the town looked beautiful to him.

   > 他说他来自印度尼西亚，并且这个城镇在他看来很美。
   >

40. He liked the river, the old buildings, and the small shops near the front gate.

   > 他喜欢河流、老建筑和前门附近的小商店。
   >

41. His first home was with a local family.

   > 他最初住在一个当地家庭家里。
   >

42. The family were warm and patient, and they often invited him to dinner.

   > 那家人热情又有耐心，并且经常邀请他一起吃晚饭。
   >

43. But they had three young children, so the house was never quiet.

   > 但是他们有三个小孩，所以房子里从不安静。
   >

44. Toys lay on the floor, and the kitchen table was often messy.

   > 玩具散落在地板上，厨房桌子也常常很乱。
   >

45. Luke could not study well there, although he liked the family.

   > 卢克在那里学不好，尽管他喜欢那家人。
   >

46. After three months, he moved into a house with two other students.

   > 三个月后，他搬进了一所房子，和另外两名学生合住。
   >

47. The house was cheaper, and the students could share cooking and cleaning.

   > 那所房子更便宜，学生们可以分担做饭和打扫。
   >

48. To make the rooms better, they went to a small student auction.

   > 为了让房间更好一些，他们去参加了一场小型学生拍卖。
   >

49. A seller there offered old chairs, plates, and a strong shelf.

   > 那里的一位卖家出售旧椅子、盘子和一个结实的架子。
   >

50. A crowd stood around the tables, but Luke waited without speaking.

   > 一群人围在桌子周围，但卢克没有说话，只是等着。
   >

51. He bought the shelf for a low price and carried it home with the other students.

   > 他以低价买下那个架子，并和另外两名学生一起把它搬回家。
   >

52. Now the front room of their house was no longer empty.

   > 现在他们房子的前屋不再空荡荡了。
   >

53. Books, cups, and small plants stood on the shelf.

   > 书、杯子和小植物放在架子上。
   >

54. Mary then asked Luke about his studies.

   > 然后玛丽问卢克关于学习的情况。
   >

55. He said the main problem was not the lessons but the computer room.

   > 他说主要问题不是课程，而是电脑室。
   >

56. There was always a crowd waiting in front of the door.

   > 门前总是有一群人在等。
   >

57. Sometimes he walked there after dinner and found every computer already in use.

   > 有时他晚饭后走到那里，却发现每台电脑都已经有人在用。
   >

58. He wanted students to reserve a time, so they could plan their work.

   > 他希望学生可以预约时间，这样他们就能安排自己的学习。
   >

59. Mary saw that Kate and Luke had different problems, but both needed better support.

   > 玛丽看出凯特和卢克的问题不同，但两人都需要更好的支持。
   >

60. When the meeting ended, her guide had a simple message: new students need rooms, time, and people who help them feel less alone.

   > 会议结束时，她的指南有了一个简单的信息：新学生需要住处、时间，以及能帮助他们不那么孤单的人。
   >
