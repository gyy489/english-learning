# 第 42 天中译英：为新生指南收集真实建议

## 中文题目

请将下面的小故事翻译成英文，尽量使用建议词汇。

在剑桥的学生服务办公室，玛丽准备为即将到来的海外新生写一本实用指南。她不想只列出冷冰冰的规定，于是请已经适应了一段时间的学生来讲真实经历。那天下午，会议室几乎空荡荡的，前窗旁只有几把椅子，墙边还有一个落了灰的架子。

凯特先来了。她说，刚到这里时，晚上街上安静得让她不习惯；在她的家乡，天黑后外面仍常有一群人。虽然她的宿舍包饭，也住满了学生，但大家只是点头问好，很少真正交流。她的房间很小，东西很快堆满了架子，包里的笔记也乱糟糟的。她还希望老师能定期和学生见面，因为有趣的课程并不一定让新生感到有人支持。玛丽匆忙记下“帮助学生参与交流”这个要点时，还把其中一个词少写了一个字母，后来才改正。

后来，卢克也来了。他喜欢校门前的小店和古老建筑，但他最初住的当地家庭有三个孩子，家里总是吵闹，厨房桌子也常常凌乱。虽然那家人很友好，他还是搬去和两个学生合租。为了布置客厅，他们去参加了一场学生旧物拍卖；桌子周围挤着一群人，一个卖家正在出售旧椅子、盘子和结实的架子。卢克低价买下架子后，客厅终于不再空荡。最后，玛丽明白：新生不仅需要住处和学习时间，也需要能让他们真正融入的人和机会。

#### 网页订正

```text
At the student services office in Cambridge, Mary was preparing to write a practical guide for new overseas students. She did not want to list only cold rules, so she invited some students who had already got used to life there to share their real stories. That afternoon, the meeting room was almost empty. There were only a few chairs by the front window, and there was a shelf by the wall, covered with dust.

Kate arrived first. She said that when she first arrived, the quiet streets at night felt strange to her. In her home city, there was often a crowd outside even after dark. Although her student hall included meals and was full of students, people usually only nodded and said hello; they rarely really talked with each other. Her room was very small, so her things soon filled the shelf, and the notes in her bag were messy. She also hoped that teachers could meet students regularly, because interesting lessons do not always make new students feel supported. As Mary quickly wrote down the point, "help students engage with each other," she missed one letter in one word, but she corrected it later.

Later, Luke arrived too. He liked the small shops in front of the school gate and the old buildings, but the local family he first lived with had three children, so the home was always loud, and the kitchen table was often messy. Although the family was friendly, he still moved out and shared a place with two students. To set up the living room, they went to a student second-hand auction. A crowd had gathered around a table, and a seller was selling old chairs, plates, and a strong shelf. Luke bought the shelf at a low price, and after that, the living room no longer felt empty. In the end, Mary understood that new students need not only a place to live and time to study, but also people and chances that help them truly engage with others.
```

#### 反馈

- 整体内容完整，但原文有较多主谓一致和时态问题，如 has/was come/live 等，已改为自然的过去时表达。
- although 后面不要再接 but；英文中通常只保留 although 或 but 其中一个。
- engage 常用搭配是 engage with someone 或 engage in something，不能直接说 never really engage。
- 一些直译表达已调整为更自然的英文，如 room meeting → meeting room，beside wall → by the wall。
- 已覆盖玛丽写指南、邀请学生分享经历以及会议室环境。
- 已覆盖凯特对安静街道、宿舍交流少、房间小和希望老师定期见面的感受。
- 已覆盖卢克的寄宿家庭、搬家、参加旧物拍卖、买架子以及玛丽最后的理解。
