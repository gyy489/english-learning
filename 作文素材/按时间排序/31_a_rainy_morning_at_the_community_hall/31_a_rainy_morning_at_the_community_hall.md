# A Rainy Morning at the Community Hall

- 天数：第 31 天
- 来源真题：无（纯生词复习）
- 来源文件：无（纯生词复习）
- 复习内容：D30 当前生词 + 到期旧词 + 积压清理（纯单词）

- rope - 绳子
- contrast - 对比
- describe - 描述
- ride - 骑行；乘坐
- wheel - 轮子
- paragraph - 段落
- reach - 到达；伸手够到
- dry - 干的；使变干

## 正文

1. After a night of heavy rain, the roof of the small community hall was still wet.

   > 一夜大雨之后，小社区礼堂的屋顶仍然是湿的。
   >

2. I could hear the steady sound of water dropping into a plastic bucket near the door.

   > 我能听见门边水不断滴进塑料桶里的声音。
   >

3. At first, I had some doubt about whether our study group should meet there.

   > 起初，我有些怀疑我们的学习小组是否应该在那里见面。
   >

4. However, the room was warm, and the tables were dry enough for us to use.

   > 然而，房间很暖和，桌子也足够干，可以让我们使用。
   >

5. My friend Leo arrived late because the front wheel of his bike had got stuck in the mud.

   > 我的朋友 Leo 迟到了，因为他自行车的前轮陷在了泥里。
   >

6. He said the ride across the park had been slower than usual, but it was still peaceful.

   > 他说穿过公园的骑行比平时慢，但仍然很平静。
   >

7. To stop the door from moving in the wind, we tied it with a rope.

   > 为了防止门被风吹动，我们用一根绳子把它系住。
   >

8. The contrast between the storm outside and the quiet room inside made everyone feel calmer.

   > 外面的暴风雨和里面安静的房间形成对比，让每个人都感到更平静。
   >

9. Our teacher asked us to describe the morning in five clear sentences.

   > 老师让我们用五个清楚的句子描述这个早晨。
   >

10. I wrote one paragraph about the leaking roof and another about the people helping each other.

   > 我写了一段关于漏水屋顶的内容，又写了一段关于人们互相帮助的内容。
   >

11. Although there was some uncertainty about the weather, nobody wanted to cancel the lesson.

   > 虽然天气有些不确定，但没有人想取消这节课。
   >

12. The teacher did not force us to stay, but she encouraged us to make a careful choice.

   > 老师没有强迫我们留下，但她鼓励我们认真做出选择。
   >

13. I did not want to disappoint her, so I decided to finish my writing task.

   > 我不想让她失望，所以我决定完成写作任务。
   >

14. When I tried to reach my notebook on the high shelf, Leo stood up to help me.

   > 当我想够到高架子上的笔记本时，Leo 站起来帮我。
   >

15. His jacket was wet, but he laughed and said it would soon dry beside the heater.

   > 他的夹克是湿的，但他笑着说它很快会在暖气旁边变干。
   >

16. We moved our chairs closer together because the back corner of the room felt cold.

   > 我们把椅子移得更近一些，因为房间后面的角落感觉很冷。
   >

17. The sound of rain became softer, and our discussion became more focused.

   > 雨声变小了，我们的讨论也变得更专注。
   >

18. One student said bad weather often shows the contrast between planning and real life.

   > 一位学生说，坏天气常常显示出计划和现实生活之间的对比。
   >

19. Another student described how her father checks the roof every autumn before the rainy season.

   > 另一位学生描述了她父亲如何在每年秋天雨季前检查屋顶。
   >

20. I liked that idea because it connected a simple home problem with responsibility.

   > 我喜欢那个想法，因为它把一个简单的家庭问题和责任联系起来。
   >

21. During the break, we shared tea and watched the street slowly become less wet.

   > 休息时，我们一起喝茶，看着街道慢慢变得不那么湿。
   >

22. A delivery worker pushed a cart past the window, and one wheel made a sharp noise.

   > 一名送货员推着手推车从窗前经过，其中一个轮子发出刺耳的声音。
   >

23. Leo said the noise reminded him to repair his own bike before the next ride.

   > Leo 说这个声音提醒他在下次骑行前修理自己的自行车。
   >

24. By noon, the rain had stopped, but there was still a feeling of uncertainty in the sky.

   > 到中午时，雨已经停了，但天空中仍有一种不确定的感觉。
   >

25. The teacher asked us not to doubt our ability just because the morning had been difficult.

   > 老师让我们不要因为这个早晨很困难就怀疑自己的能力。
   >

26. She said useful writing often comes from ordinary moments that we observe carefully.

   > 她说，有用的写作常常来自我们认真观察的普通时刻。
   >

27. Her words gave the group new energy, without any force or pressure.

   > 她的话给了小组新的动力，没有任何强迫或压力。
   >

28. Before leaving, we cleaned the floor and placed the wet towels where they could dry.

   > 离开前，我们清理了地板，并把湿毛巾放在可以晾干的地方。
   >

29. I felt glad that the strange morning did not disappoint us in the end.

   > 我很高兴这个奇怪的早晨最终没有让我们失望。
   >

30. On my ride home, I planned to turn the whole experience into a longer paragraph for homework.

   > 在骑车回家的路上，我打算把整个经历写成一段更长的家庭作业。
   >
