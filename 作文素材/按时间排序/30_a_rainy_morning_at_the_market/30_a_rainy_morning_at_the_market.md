# A Rainy Morning at the Market

- 天数：第 30 天
- 来源真题：无（纯生词复习）
- 来源文件：无（纯生词复习）

## 正文

1. On Saturday morning, Daniel walked along the main road to the local market.

   > 星期六早上，丹尼尔沿着主路走向当地市场。
   >

2. He usually went there to buy fruit, but this time he wanted to understand how small trade worked in his town.

   > 他通常去那里买水果，但这次他想了解镇上的小买卖是如何运作的。
   >

3. The rain had hit the roofs during the night, so the air felt fresh and cool.

   > 夜里雨水敲打过屋顶，所以空气感觉清新而凉爽。
   >

4. When he reached the market gate, he saw a short chain hanging across one side of the entrance.

   > 当他到达市场大门时，他看到入口一侧挂着一条短链子。
   >

5. A notice explained that there was a new restriction on delivery carts before eight o'clock.

   > 一张通知解释说，八点前送货车有一项新限制。
   >

6. Some sellers complained quietly, but most of them simply carried their boxes by hand instead.

   > 一些卖家小声抱怨，但大多数人只是改为手提箱子。
   >

7. Daniel helped an old seller bring two baskets of oranges through the open side of the gate.

   > 丹尼尔帮一位年老的卖家把两篮橙子从大门开着的一侧搬进去。
   >

8. The seller thanked him and asked Daniel to tie a cloth cover over the baskets.

   > 那位卖家感谢他，并请丹尼尔把布盖系在篮子上。
   >

9. The knot had to be firm because a sharp wind was moving across the market square.

   > 结必须系牢，因为一阵明显的冷风正吹过市场广场。
   >

10. Gradually, more people arrived, and the quiet place became full of voices.

   > 渐渐地，更多人来了，安静的地方充满了说话声。
   >

11. Daniel noticed that each seller had a different way of attracting customers.

   > 丹尼尔注意到，每个卖家吸引顾客的方式都不同。
   >

12. One fruit seller smiled at everyone, while another spoke only when people came forward to ask a question.

   > 一个水果卖家对每个人微笑，而另一个只有在人们上前提问时才说话。
   >

13. Although the second seller seemed less friendly, his apples were fresh and his prices were fair.

   > 虽然第二个卖家看起来不那么友好，但他的苹果很新鲜，价格也公道。
   >

14. Daniel bought one apple and began to chew it while he walked between the stalls.

   > 丹尼尔买了一个苹果，一边在摊位之间走，一边咀嚼它。
   >

15. The taste was sweeter than he expected, so he went back to buy more.

   > 味道比他预想的更甜，所以他回去又买了一些。
   >

16. The seller said that good fruit did not need loud words; it could speak for itself.

   > 卖家说，好水果不需要大声宣传；它自己就能说明品质。
   >

17. Daniel thought this was a useful lesson about trade and trust.

   > 丹尼尔觉得这是关于买卖和信任的一堂有用的课。
   >

18. Near the vegetable stall, two women discussed whether the new restriction would slow business.

   > 在蔬菜摊附近，两位女士讨论新限制是否会拖慢生意。
   >

19. One woman believed it would cause trouble, but the other said it might make the market safer.

   > 一位女士认为这会带来麻烦，但另一位说它可能让市场更安全。
   >

20. Daniel could see both sides, because the road outside the market was often crowded.

   > 丹尼尔能理解双方，因为市场外面的道路经常很拥挤。
   >

21. If heavy carts rushed through the gate too early, they might hit people who were setting up their stalls.

   > 如果重推车太早冲过大门，它们可能会撞到正在摆摊的人。
   >

22. At the same time, carrying goods by hand made the sellers tired before the day even began.

   > 与此同时，手提货物让卖家在一天还没开始前就已经疲惫。
   >

23. Daniel moved forward slowly and watched how people solved small problems together.

   > 丹尼尔慢慢向前走，观察人们如何一起解决小问题。
   >

24. A young seller borrowed a rope from her neighbor to tie her boxes together.

   > 一位年轻卖家向邻摊借了一根绳子，把她的箱子绑在一起。
   >

25. Another seller used a simple chain to keep his empty crates from falling over.

   > 另一位卖家用一条简单的链子防止空箱子倒下。
   >

26. These small actions brought order to a place that first seemed rather messy.

   > 这些小举动给一个起初看起来相当混乱的地方带来了秩序。
   >

27. By noon, the sun came out, and the wet stones on the road began to dry.

   > 到了中午，太阳出来了，路上潮湿的石头开始变干。
   >

28. Daniel sat near the gate and wrote a few notes in his phone.

   > 丹尼尔坐在大门附近，在手机里写了几条笔记。
   >

29. He realized that rules, patience, and cooperation could gradually change the mood of a busy place.

   > 他意识到，规则、耐心和合作可以逐渐改变一个繁忙地方的气氛。
   >

30. When he walked home, he looked forward to telling his family what he had learned from an ordinary market morning.

   > 当他走回家时，他期待着告诉家人他从一个普通的市场早晨学到了什么。
   >
