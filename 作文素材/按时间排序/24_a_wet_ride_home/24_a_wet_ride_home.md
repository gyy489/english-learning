# A Wet Ride Home

- 天数：第 24 天
- 来源真题：无（纯生词复习）
- 来源文件：无（纯生词复习）
- 复习内容：D23 当前生词 + 到期旧词 + 积压清理（纯单词）

- ride - 骑行；乘坐

- through - 穿过；通过
- road - 道路
- reach - 到达；够到

## 正文

1. This morning, Tom planned to ride his bike to the small shop.

   > 今天早上，汤姆计划骑自行车去小商店。
   >

2. He stood by the gate and looked at the sky.

   > 他站在大门旁，看着天空。
   >

3. The road was still wet after last night's rain.

   > 昨晚下雨后，道路仍然是湿的。
   >

4. Tom did not doubt that he could go slowly and safely.

   > 汤姆不怀疑自己可以慢慢地、安全地走。
   >

5. He put a piece of bread in his mouth and began to chew.

   > 他把一片面包放进嘴里，开始咀嚼。
   >

6. Then he pushed his bike through the gate.

   > 然后他把自行车推过大门。
   >

7. His mother said, “Please communicate with me if you are late.”

   > 他的妈妈说：“如果你迟到了，请和我联系。”
   >

8. Tom nodded and started his ride.

   > 汤姆点点头，开始骑行。
   >

9. At first, the road was quiet and easy.

   > 一开始，道路很安静，也很好走。
   >

10. He passed a blank wall near the old school.

   > 他经过了旧学校旁边一面空白的墙。
   >

11. There were no pictures or words on it.

   > 上面没有图画，也没有文字。
   >

12. Suddenly, his front wheel hit a small stone.

   > 突然，他的前轮撞到了一块小石头。
   >

13. Tom held the bike with both hands.

   > 汤姆用双手握住自行车。
   >

14. He used all his force to stay on the bike.

   > 他用尽力气让自己留在自行车上。
   >

15. The bike moved to the side, but he did not fall.

   > 自行车移向一边，但他没有摔倒。
   >

16. His shoes became wet when he stepped into a small pool of water.

   > 当他踩进一小滩水里时，他的鞋子湿了。
   >

17. Tom felt cold, but he kept going.

   > 汤姆觉得冷，但他继续前进。
   >

18. The road seemed endless because he was moving so slowly.

   > 因为他走得很慢，这条路看起来无尽一样。
   >

19. He did not want to disappoint his mother.

   > 他不想让妈妈失望。
   >

20. He also wanted to reach the shop before it closed.

   > 他也想在商店关门前到达那里。
   >

21. After some time, he saw the shop at the end of the road.

   > 过了一会儿，他看到了路尽头的商店。
   >

22. He parked his bike near the door.

   > 他把自行车停在门旁边。
   >

23. The shopkeeper smiled and gave him a dry cloth.

   > 店主笑了笑，给了他一块干布。
   >

24. Tom cleaned the wet seat and checked the wheel.

   > 汤姆擦干湿座位，并检查了轮子。
   >

25. Then he bought bread, milk, and a small cake for his mother.

   > 然后他买了面包、牛奶和一个小蛋糕给妈妈。
   >

26. On the way home, he rode more carefully.

   > 在回家的路上，他骑得更小心。
   >

27. When he came through the gate again, his mother was waiting.

   > 当他再次穿过大门时，他的妈妈正在等他。
   >

28. Tom smiled and said, “I am wet, but I am home.”

   > 汤姆笑着说：“我湿了，但我到家了。”
   >
