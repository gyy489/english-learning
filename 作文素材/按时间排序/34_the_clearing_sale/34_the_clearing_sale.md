# The Clearing Sale

- 来源真题：无（纯生词复习）
- 来源文件：无（纯生词复习）

## 正文

1. Emily decided to hold a garage sale.

   > 艾米丽决定举办一次车库甩卖。
   >

2. She wanted to clear out items that were no longer worth keeping.

   > 她想清理那些不再值得保留的东西。
   >

3. Gradually, she gathered everything she wanted to sell.

   > 她逐渐地收集了所有想要出售的东西。
   >

4. She was glad when neighbors showed interest.

   > 当邻居们表现出兴趣时，她很高兴。
   >

5. There was no restriction on what could be sold.

   > 没有限制可以出售什么。
   >

6. Emily placed a sharp knife among the kitchenware.

   > 艾米丽把一把锋利的刀放在厨房用品中。
   >

7. Instead of selling individually, she offered bundles for a better trade.

   > 她没有单独出售，而是打包出售以获得更好的交易。
   >

8. A lady asked about the story behind a vintage lamp.

   > 一位女士询问关于那盏复古灯背后的故事。
   >

9. Emily explained it was a common item in her grandmother’s house.

   > 艾米丽解释说，这是她祖母家常见的物品。
   >

10. A young man decided to buy a tie that caught his eye.

    > 一位年轻人决定购买一条引起他注意的领带。
    >

11. He said he had an important ceremony to attend.

    > 他说他要参加一个重要的仪式。
    >

12. As the day went on, Emily noticed the sun starting to fall.

    > 随着一天的结束，艾米丽注意到太阳渐渐落下。
    >

13. She gently packed away items that were left unsold.

    > 她温柔地收起那些未售出的物品。
    >

14. It was a successful day, and she felt satisfied.

    > 这是个成功的一天，她感到很满足。
    >

15. The next day, she would hold another sale to clear more space.

    > 第二天，她将再次举办销售以清理更多空间。
    >

16. This time, she might invite more sellers to join.

    > 这次，她可能会邀请更多的卖家加入。
    >

17. Emily realized how the sales could connect her with others.

    > 艾米丽意识到销售如何能让她与他人联系起来。
    >

18. It was not just about making money, but also building ties.

    > 这不只是赚钱的问题，也是在建立联系。
    >

19. She was glad to discover this new aspect of community.

    > 她很高兴发现了这个社区的新方面。
    >

20. Gradually, her house felt more organized and spacious.

    > 逐渐地，她的房子变得更整洁和宽敞了。
    >

21. Emily started enjoying the process rather than seeing it as a chore.

    > 艾米丽开始享受这个过程，而不是将其视为一项繁琐的家务。
    >

22. Each item she sold had a story, which made the experience worthwhile.

    > 她出售的每件物品都有一个故事，这让整个体验很有价值。
    >

23. People came not only to buy but also to share their own stories.

    > 人们来到这里不仅仅是为了购买，还为了分享他们自己的故事。
    >

24. Emily realized how common it was to hold such events.

    > 艾米丽意识到举办这种活动是多么常见。
    >

25. Behind each sale was a chance for connection and understanding.

    > 在每次销售背后都是一次连接和理解的机会。
    >

26. Emily was planning the next sale with a keen sense of anticipation.

    > 艾米丽怀着强烈的期待感计划着下一次销售。
    >

27. She knew it would not only clear space but also bring joy.

    > 她知道这不仅能清理空间，还能带来快乐。
    >

28. Emily realized that sometimes what we hold onto can be shared instead for greater happiness.

    > 艾米丽意识到，有时候我们所保留的东西可以分享以获取更大的快乐。
    >
