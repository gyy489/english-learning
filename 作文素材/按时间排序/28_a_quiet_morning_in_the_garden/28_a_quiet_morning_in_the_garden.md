# A Quiet Morning in the Garden

- 天数：第 28 天
- 来源真题：无（纯生词复习）
- 来源文件：无（纯生词复习）
- 复习内容：D27 当前生词 + 到期旧词 + 积压清理（纯单词）

## 正文

1. On Saturday morning, I visited my aunt in her small village.

   > 星期六早上，我去小村庄看望我的阿姨。
   >

2. She greeted me warmly at the door.

   > 她在门口热情地迎接我。
   >

3. My aunt is a religious woman, and she often goes to a small church near her home.

   > 我的阿姨是一位虔诚的女性，她经常去家附近的一座小教堂。
   >

4. After breakfast, she asked me to help in the garden.

   > 早饭后，她让我在花园里帮忙。
   >

5. The garden was quiet, but there was a lot of work to do.

   > 花园很安静，但有很多活要做。
   >

6. The ground near the gate was uneven, so we walked slowly.

   > 大门附近的地面不平坦，所以我们走得很慢。
   >

7. A wooden fence stood around the garden.

   > 一道木栅栏围着花园。
   >

8. Some parts of the fence were old and loose.

   > 栅栏的一些地方又旧又松。
   >

9. My aunt said strong wind had broken one branch of the apple tree.

   > 阿姨说大风折断了苹果树的一根树枝。
   >

10. We picked up the branch and put it beside the wall.

   > 我们捡起那根树枝，把它放在墙边。
   >

11. Then we added fresh soil around the flowers.

   > 然后我们在花周围加了新土。
   >

12. I used a small spoon because the flower roots were weak.

   > 我用了一把小勺子，因为花根很脆弱。
   >

13. My aunt told me not to use too much water, because it could be harmful.

   > 阿姨告诉我不要用太多水，因为那可能有害。
   >

14. Suddenly, we heard a noise behind the fence.

   > 突然，我们听到栅栏后面有声音。
   >

15. A little dog came in through a small hole.

   > 一只小狗从一个小洞钻了进来。
   >

16. It was carrying an old bone in its mouth.

   > 它嘴里叼着一根旧骨头。
   >

17. My aunt laughed and said the dog often visited her garden.

   > 阿姨笑着说这只狗经常来她的花园。
   >

18. I wanted to send it away, but she told me not to argue with a hungry dog.

   > 我想把它赶走，但她告诉我不要和一只饿狗较劲。
   >

19. We gave the dog some clean water in a round bowl.

   > 我们用一个圆碗给那只狗倒了些干净的水。
   >

20. It drank quickly and then sat under the tree.

   > 它很快喝完水，然后坐在树下。
   >

21. Later, my aunt made tea in the kitchen.

   > 后来，阿姨在厨房里泡茶。
   >

22. She asked me to stir the tea with a spoon.

   > 她让我用勺子搅拌茶。
   >

23. While we drank tea, she showed me an old family record.

   > 我们喝茶时，她给我看了一份旧的家庭记录。
   >

24. The record had names, dates, and short stories about our family.

   > 那份记录里有名字、日期和关于我们家的短故事。
   >

25. I read it slowly and asked her many simple questions.

   > 我慢慢读着，并问了她很多简单的问题。
   >

26. She answered kindly and did not feel tired.

   > 她亲切地回答，并没有感到累。
   >

27. In the afternoon, we fixed the hole in the fence together.

   > 下午，我们一起修好了栅栏上的洞。
   >

28. When I left, the garden looked cleaner, and my aunt smiled warmly again.

   > 我离开时，花园看起来更干净了，阿姨又温暖地笑了。
   >
