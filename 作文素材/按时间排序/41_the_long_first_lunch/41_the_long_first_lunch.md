# The Long First Lunch

- 天数：第 41 天
- 来源真题：Cambridge IELTS 1 Listening Test 4 / Section 1

- aware - 意识到的

- religious - 宗教的

## 正文

1. On Monday morning, Anna arrived at the university with an empty stomach and a heavy bag.

   > 星期一早上，安娜空着肚子，背着沉重的包来到大学。
   >

2. She had to register for her first year, but she did not know the right area.

   > 她必须注册第一年的课程，但她不知道正确的区域。
   >

3. A tall student stopped near the gate and looked just as lost.

   > 一个高个学生在门口附近停下来，看起来也一样迷路。
   >

4. His name was Tom, and he was also trying to find the main office.

   > 他叫汤姆，也在努力寻找主办公室。
   >

5. They smiled because the problem was simple but also a little funny.

   > 他们笑了，因为这个问题很简单，但也有点好笑。
   >

6. A campus map had been put on an old fence near the entrance.

   > 校园地图被放在入口附近的一道旧栅栏上。
   >

7. The map showed a library, a food hall, a religious centre, and several teaching buildings.

   > 地图上显示了图书馆、餐厅、宗教中心和几栋教学楼。
   >

8. The main office was marked in a small square at the back of the area.

   > 主办公室被标在这个区域后方的一个小方块里。
   >

9. Anna was aware that they could waste the whole morning if they chose the wrong path.

   > 安娜意识到，如果他们选错路，可能会浪费整个上午。
   >

10. Tom pointed to a line of uneven steps leading up the hill.

   > 汤姆指向一排通往小山上的不平台阶。
   >

11. They climbed slowly, because many new students were carrying bags and papers.

   > 他们慢慢往上走，因为许多新生都拿着包和文件。
   >

12. At the top, they found a wide hall, but most desks were empty.

   > 到了上面，他们发现一个宽大厅，但大多数桌子都是空的。
   >

13. For a moment, Anna thought they had come to the wrong place.

   > 有一会儿，安娜以为他们走错了地方。
   >

14. Then a small sign sent them through another door.

   > 然后一个小标牌指引他们穿过另一扇门。
   >

15. Behind it, the line was much longer than expected.

   > 在门后，队伍比预想的长得多。
   >

16. Tom checked his watch and said they might still be there at two.

   > 汤姆看了看表，说他们可能到两点还在那里。
   >

17. Anna suddenly remembered that she had not had breakfast.

   > 安娜突然想起自己没有吃早餐。
   >

18. Her bag felt more heavy, and the hall seemed more warm.

   > 她的包感觉更重了，大厅也似乎更热了。
   >

19. Tom said he could stay in the line if she went to buy lunch.

   > 汤姆说，如果她去买午饭，他可以留在队伍里。
   >

20. She agreed, because it was a fair way to share the waiting.

   > 她同意了，因为这是分担等待的公平方式。
   >

21. Before she left, Tom asked for something simple from the food hall.

   > 她离开前，汤姆要了一份餐厅里的简单食物。
   >

22. He first wanted rice, then changed his mind and asked for bread with cheese.

   > 他一开始想要米饭，后来改变主意，要了夹奶酪的面包。
   >

23. Anna wrote it down so there would be a clear record.

   > 安娜把它写下来，这样就会有清楚的记录。
   >

24. He also wanted juice, because hot coffee would be hard to carry.

   > 他还想要果汁，因为热咖啡很难拿。
   >

25. He gave her some money and told her not to worry about the change.

   > 他给了她一些钱，并告诉她不用担心零钱。
   >

26. The food hall was in another area near the sports field.

   > 餐厅在运动场附近的另一个区域。
   >

27. Anna followed the signs and tried to explore the campus as she walked.

   > 安娜一边走一边跟着标志，试着探索校园。
   >

28. She passed the religious centre and a quiet garden with blue seats.

   > 她经过宗教中心和一个有蓝色座椅的安静花园。
   >

29. The food hall looked open, but there was an unexpected restriction at the door.

   > 餐厅看起来开着，但门口有一个意外的限制。
   >

30. Only students with new ID papers could enter by the front door.

   > 只有拿着新身份证明的学生才能从前门进入。
   >

31. Anna had no ID yet, so she had to use the side door.

   > 安娜还没有身份证明，所以她必须走侧门。
   >

32. Inside, the room was full of voices and moving plates.

   > 里面满是说话声和移动的盘子。
   >

33. She tried to engage with the woman at the counter, but the woman was too busy.

   > 她试着和柜台的女士交流，但那位女士太忙了。
   >

34. By the time Anna reached the front, the bread shelf was almost empty.

   > 等安娜到前面时，面包架几乎空了。
   >

35. There was no cheese bread left.

   > 已经没有奶酪面包了。
   >

36. Anna chose some rice, an apple, and a bottle of orange juice.

   > 安娜选了一些米饭、一个苹果和一瓶橙汁。
   >

37. She felt sorry, but she knew food was better than an excuse.

   > 她感到抱歉，但她知道有食物总比有借口好。
   >

38. When she returned, Tom had moved only a few steps forward.

   > 当她回来时，汤姆只向前移动了几步。
   >

39. He looked tired, but he laughed when he saw the rice.

   > 他看起来很累，但看到米饭时笑了。
   >

40. Anna explained the empty shelf and the side-door rule.

   > 安娜解释了空货架和侧门规定。
   >

41. Tom said it was fine because he was hungry enough to eat anything.

   > 汤姆说没关系，因为他饿到什么都能吃。
   >

42. He offered to pay her back, but she told him to buy her coffee later.

   > 他提出还她钱，但她让他之后给她买咖啡。
   >

43. The small joke helped them engage more easily.

   > 这个小玩笑让他们更容易熟络起来。
   >

44. Soon they reached the desk, where a man in a blue tie asked for Anna's name.

   > 很快他们到了桌前，一位戴蓝色领带的男士问安娜的名字。
   >

45. The man made a record of her address and course.

   > 那位男士记录了她的地址和课程。
   >

46. Anna said her flat phone was not ready yet.

   > 安娜说她公寓里的电话还没装好。
   >

47. The man nodded and wrote that the number would come later.

   > 那位男士点点头，并写下电话号码之后再补。
   >

48. Then he told them the first office was not the final stop.

   > 然后他告诉他们，第一个办公室并不是最后一站。
   >

49. They had to take a card to the law building for an official mark.

   > 他们必须把一张卡拿到法律学院楼盖一个正式标记。
   >

50. After that, they had to return and pay a student fee.

   > 之后，他们还得回来交学生费用。
   >

51. Tom looked at Anna and said this was a sharp lesson in university life.

   > 汤姆看着安娜说，这是大学生活中一个直接而深刻的教训。
   >

52. Nothing was truly hard, but every small step was tied to another small step.

   > 没有什么真的很难，但每一个小步骤都和另一个小步骤相连。
   >

53. Anna put the card carefully in her bag so it would stay smooth.

   > 安娜小心地把卡放进包里，好让它保持平整。
   >

54. Outside, the air felt less warm than the crowded hall.

   > 外面的空气没有拥挤的大厅那么热。
   >

55. They sat under a tree near the fence and shared the short lunch.

   > 他们坐在栅栏附近的一棵树下，吃了这顿简短的午饭。
   >

56. The rice was not what Tom had chosen, but it made him feel better.

   > 米饭不是汤姆原本选择的食物，但让他感觉好多了。
   >

57. Anna had some water and looked again at the map.

   > 安娜喝了点水，又看了看地图。
   >

58. This time, the campus did not seem so hard to understand.

   > 这一次，校园看起来没有那么难懂了。
   >

59. They planned to reach the law building by one sharp.

   > 他们计划在一点整到达法律学院楼。
   >

60. Before leaving, Anna said their first morning had been messy but useful.

   > 离开前，安娜说他们的第一个上午虽然混乱，但很有用。
   >

61. Tom agreed, because they now knew where to go, what to ask, and how to wait.

   > 汤姆同意了，因为他们现在知道该去哪里、该问什么、以及如何等待。
   >
