# A Careful Plan for the Weekend Market

- 来源真题：无（纯生词复习）

## 正文

1. On Saturday morning, Maya helped her uncle prepare a small food stall for the weekend market.

   > 周六早上，玛雅帮她叔叔为周末市场准备一个小食品摊。
   >

2. The road outside their house was uneven, so they had to push the cart slowly.

   > 他们家外面的路不平坦，所以他们不得不慢慢推车。
   >

3. Her uncle had been ill the week before and still felt a little weak.

   > 她叔叔前一周生病了，现在仍然觉得有点虚弱。
   >

4. Because of this, Maya carried the heavier boxes and checked every bag carefully.

   > 因此，玛雅搬较重的箱子，并仔细检查每个袋子。
   >

5. She was aware that one small mistake could waste both time and money.

   > 她意识到，一个小错误可能会浪费时间和金钱。
   >

6. At the market gate, a new restriction allowed each stall to use only one table.

   > 在市场入口，一项新规定只允许每个摊位使用一张桌子。
   >

7. Some sellers began to argue with the manager, but Maya’s uncle stayed calm.

   > 一些卖家开始和管理员争论，但玛雅的叔叔保持冷静。
   >

8. He said it was better to solve the problem than to make the morning more stressful.

   > 他说，与其让早晨变得更有压力，不如解决问题。
   >

9. Maya helped him tie a blue cloth around the table legs to make the stall look neat.

   > 玛雅帮他把一块蓝布系在桌腿周围，让摊位看起来整洁。
   >

10. Behind them, an old wooden fence separated the market from a quiet garden.

   > 在他们身后，一道旧木栅栏把市场和一个安静的花园隔开。
   >

11. The fence was not beautiful, yet it made the place feel safer and more organized.

   > 这道栅栏并不漂亮，但它让这个地方感觉更安全、更有秩序。
   >

12. Maya used a large spoon to stir the soup while her uncle arranged the bread.

   > 玛雅用一把大勺子搅拌汤，而她叔叔摆放面包。
   >

13. The smell attracted several customers before the stall was fully ready.

   > 在摊位完全准备好之前，香味就吸引了几位顾客。
   >

14. One customer asked whether the soup was too salty or harmful for children.

   > 一位顾客问这汤是否太咸，或者对孩子有害。
   >

15. Maya explained that they used fresh vegetables and only a small amount of salt.

   > 玛雅解释说，他们用了新鲜蔬菜，而且只放了少量盐。
   >

16. Her uncle kept a record of each sale in a small notebook.

   > 她叔叔在一个小笔记本里记录每一笔销售。
   >

17. He believed that a clear record helped them understand what people really wanted.

   > 他认为，清楚的记录能帮助他们了解人们真正想要什么。
   >

18. Around noon, the weather changed unpredictably, and a cold wind moved through the market.

   > 中午左右，天气不可预测地变了，一阵冷风吹过市场。
   >

19. Maya held the sign tightly because its metal edge was sharp.

   > 玛雅紧紧抓住招牌，因为它的金属边缘很锋利。
   >

20. If it fell, it might hurt someone or damage another stall.

   > 如果它掉下来，可能会伤到别人，或者损坏另一个摊位。
   >

21. A fruit seller nearby offered to trade two apples for a bowl of soup.

   > 附近的一位水果摊主提出用两个苹果换一碗汤。
   >

22. Maya’s uncle accepted the trade because both sides were pleased with it.

   > 玛雅的叔叔接受了这次交换，因为双方都很满意。
   >

23. Later, Maya noticed that fewer people stopped when the menu was written in small letters.

   > 后来，玛雅注意到，当菜单字写得很小时，停下来的人更少。
   >

24. She suggested writing the prices more clearly and placing the sign higher.

   > 她建议把价格写得更清楚，并把招牌放得更高。
   >

25. Her uncle first thought the idea was unnecessary, but he listened instead of arguing.

   > 她叔叔起初觉得这个想法没必要，但他选择倾听，而不是争论。
   >

26. After they changed the sign, more customers came to the table.

   > 他们换了招牌之后，更多顾客来到桌前。
   >

27. This small improvement made Maya feel proud because it came from careful observation.

   > 这个小小的改善让玛雅感到自豪，因为它来自仔细观察。
   >

28. By late afternoon, her uncle no longer looked as tired as he had in the morning.

   > 到下午晚些时候，她叔叔看起来不像早上那么累了。
   >

29. He said the market had taught them patience, planning, and respect for simple details.

   > 他说，这个市场教会了他们耐心、计划，以及对简单细节的重视。
   >

30. Maya agreed, and she felt ready to help again the next weekend.

   > 玛雅同意了，并且觉得自己准备好下个周末再次帮忙。
   >
