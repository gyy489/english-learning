# A Small Step Forward

- 天数：第 22 天
- 来源真题：无（纯生词复习）
- 来源文件：无（纯生词复习）
- 复习内容：D21 当前生词 + 到期旧词 + 积压清理（纯单词）

## 复习生词

- reach - 到达；够到
- tie - 系；绑
- chew - 咀嚼
- sharp - 锋利的；急转的
- restriction - 限制；规定
- unpredictably - 不可预测地
- forward - 向前
- improvement - 改进；进步
- road - 道路
- pronunciation - 发音
- whole - 整个的
- bring - 带来；拿来
- chain - 链条
- hit - 碰撞；击中
- ignore - 忽视；不理会

## 正文

1. Leo woke up early and decided to practise his English pronunciation.

   > 利奥很早醒来，决定练习英语发音。
   >

2. He read a whole page aloud before breakfast.

   > 他在早餐前大声朗读了整整一页。
   >

3. He could hear a small improvement in his voice.

   > 他能听出自己的声音有了一点进步。
   >

4. His teacher had told him to bring his notebook to class.

   > 老师曾告诉他把笔记本带到课堂上。
   >

5. Leo put it in his bag and tied his shoes.

   > 利奥把它放进包里，并系好鞋带。
   >

6. He ate some bread and chewed each piece slowly.

   > 他吃了一些面包，慢慢咀嚼每一块。
   >

7. Then he used a sharp knife to cut an apple.

   > 然后，他用一把锋利的刀切苹果。
   >

8. His phone rang, but he chose to ignore it.

   > 他的手机响了，但他选择不理会。
   >

9. He picked up his bag and walked forward to the door.

   > 他拿起书包，向前走到门口。
   >

10. The road outside was quiet in the early morning.

   > 清晨，外面的道路很安静。
   >

11. Leo planned to ride his bicycle, but its chain was loose.

   > 利奥原本打算骑自行车，但车链松了。
   >

12. He tried to fix the chain with his hands.

   > 他试着用手修理车链。
   >

13. While he was working, a small piece of metal hit the ground.

   > 在他修理时，一小块金属掉落并撞到了地面。
   >

14. He found it and put the chain back in place.

   > 他找到了它，并把车链装回原位。
   >

15. Soon, he was moving along the road again.

   > 很快，他又沿着道路前进了。
   >

16. The weather changed unpredictably, and rain began to fall.

   > 天气不可预测地发生了变化，雨开始下了起来。
   >

17. Leo moved forward more carefully because the road was wet.

   > 因为道路湿了，利奥更加小心地向前骑。
   >

18. There was a restriction on bicycles near the school gate.

   > 学校门口附近有一项针对自行车的限制规定。
   >

19. Leo had to get off his bicycle before he could reach the gate.

   > 利奥必须先下车，才能到达校门。
   >

20. He tied the bicycle to a post with a strong chain.

   > 他用一条结实的链条把自行车锁在柱子上。
   >

21. Then he ran inside and reached his classroom on time.

   > 然后，他跑进学校并准时到达了教室。
   >

22. During the lesson, the teacher asked everyone to read aloud.

   > 上课时，老师让每个人大声朗读。
   >

23. Leo made one pronunciation mistake, but he did not stop.

   > 利奥犯了一个发音错误，但他没有停下来。
   >

24. He listened carefully and repeated the whole sentence.

   > 他认真听，并重复了整个句子。
   >

25. His second try showed clear improvement.

   > 他的第二次尝试表现出了明显的进步。
   >

26. The teacher told him to keep moving forward.

   > 老师告诉他要继续向前进步。
   >

27. Leo smiled because he knew that he could reach his goal with daily practice.

   > 利奥笑了，因为他知道通过每天练习，自己能够实现目标。
   >

28. On his way home, he remembered to bring an umbrella for the next unpredictable day.

   > 在回家的路上，他记得要为下一个天气难以预测的日子带一把伞。
   >
