# A Campus Visit Last Sunday

- 天数：第 45 天
- 来源真题：Cambridge IELTS 1 Listening Test 4 / Section 3
- 来源文件：雅思真题/用于雅思学习skills数据/超给的资料/listening/cambridge-ielts-1/test4.md
- 听力片段：cambridge-ielts-1-test4-section3
- 复习内容：D44 当前生词 + 到期旧词 + 积压清理（听力情节复习）

## 复习生词

- burn - 烧伤；烧痕；燃烧
- authentic - 真实的；真正的
- claim - 声称；说法；要求
- task - 任务
- uncertainty - 不确定
- contrast - 对比
- trade - 权衡；交易；贸易
- empty - 空的
- gradually - 逐渐地
- instead - 反而；代替
- engag - engage 的误拼/不完整形式
- engage - 使参与；吸引
- front - 前面；正面
- messy - 杂乱的
- shelf - 架子

## 正文

1. Last Sunday, a student went to the campus with her friend.

   > 上周日，一名学生和她的朋友去了校园。
   >

2. They arrived at the front gate before nine o’clock.

   > 他们在九点前到达了正门。
   >

3. A student guide welcomed them near the map board.

   > 一名学生导游在地图牌旁欢迎了他们。
   >

4. The girl found a small note about a science open day.

   > 这个女孩发现了一张关于科学开放日的小纸条。
   >

5. Her friend read the note and smiled at her.

   > 她的朋友读了那张纸条，并对她笑了笑。
   >

6. Their teacher had given them a difficult task on Friday.

   > 他们的老师在周五给了他们一个困难的任务。
   >

7. The task was to prepare a short talk about an everyday object.

   > 这个任务是准备一个关于日常物品的简短演讲。
   >

8. The girl chose the campus visit because it felt more authentic than an online search.

   > 这个女孩选择了这次校园参观，因为它感觉比网上搜索更真实。
   >

9. Her friend agreed and put his phone back into his bag.

   > 她的朋友同意了，并把手机放回了包里。
   >

10. At first, they felt some uncertainty about the topic.

   > 一开始，他们对主题感到一些不确定。
   >

11. The girl wanted to talk about a water bottle.

   > 这个女孩想谈一个水瓶。
   >

12. Her friend wanted to talk about a coffee cup instead.

   > 她的朋友反而想谈一个咖啡杯。
   >

13. They walked into the science building and saw an empty drink can on a table.

   > 他们走进科学楼，看见桌上有一个空饮料罐。
   >

14. The girl picked up the empty can and looked at its side.

   > 这个女孩拿起那个空罐子，并看了看它的侧面。
   >

15. Her friend found a science board next to it.

   > 她的朋友发现它旁边有一块科学展示板。
   >

16. The board made a clear claim about the can.

   > 那块板提出了一个关于罐子的清楚说法。
   >

17. It claimed that a very light can could still hold strong pressure.

   > 它声称，一个很轻的罐子仍然能承受很强的压力。
   >

18. The girl counted three pictures on the board.

   > 这个女孩数了板上的三张图片。
   >

19. Her friend counted five short facts under the pictures.

   > 她的朋友数了图片下面的五条简短事实。
   >

20. A science teacher noticed them and walked over.

   > 一位科学老师注意到了他们，并走了过来。
   >

21. The teacher explained why the can was thin but strong.

   > 老师解释了为什么这个罐子很薄却很结实。
   >

22. She explained the trade between using less metal and keeping enough strength.

   > 她解释了少用金属和保持足够强度之间的权衡。
   >

23. The girl wrote the word trade in her notes.

   > 这个女孩在笔记里写下了 trade 这个词。
   >

24. Her friend drew the front of the can on a clean page.

   > 她的朋友在一页干净的纸上画了罐子的正面。
   >

25. The teacher pointed to the bottom of the can.

   > 老师指了指罐子的底部。
   >

26. She said the shape helped the can stay strong.

   > 她说这个形状帮助罐子保持坚固。
   >

27. The girl noticed a contrast between the thin side and the strong base.

   > 这个女孩注意到了薄薄的侧面和坚固底部之间的对比。
   >

28. Her friend added the contrast to their plan.

   > 她的朋友把这个对比加进了他们的计划。
   >

29. Then the teacher showed them a small burn mark on the work table.

   > 然后老师给他们看了工作台上的一个小烧痕。
   >

30. She explained that the burn came from an old student project, not from the can.

   > 她解释说，这个烧痕来自一个旧学生项目，不是来自那个罐子。
   >

31. The girl laughed because she had worried that the can might burn in the test.

   > 这个女孩笑了，因为她刚才担心罐子可能会在测试中燃烧。
   >

32. The teacher moved her head from side to side and smiled.

   > 老师摇了摇头并笑了。
   >

33. She told them that safety rules came before any good idea.

   > 她告诉他们，安全规则先于任何好主意。
   >

34. Her friend checked the can again and found no sharp edge.

   > 她的朋友再次检查了罐子，并没有发现锋利的边缘。
   >

35. The girl put the can on a shelf next to other sample objects.

   > 这个女孩把罐子放在其他样品物品旁边的架子上。
   >

36. The shelf looked messy, but each object had a small card.

   > 那个架子看起来很杂乱，但每个物品都有一张小卡片。
   >

37. The girl found one messy card with the word “engag” on it.

   > 这个女孩发现了一张杂乱的卡片，上面写着 “engag”。
   >

38. The teacher corrected the card and wrote “engage” clearly.

   > 老师改正了那张卡片，并清楚地写下了 “engage”。
   >

39. She explained that a good talk should engage the people listening.

   > 她解释说，一个好的演讲应该吸引听众参与。
   >

40. Her friend marked engage in his notes.

   > 她的朋友在笔记中标出了 engage。
   >

41. A visiting expert welcomed the group into a small room.

   > 一位来访专家把这组人欢迎进了一个小房间。
   >

42. He showed them a larger model of a drink can.

   > 他给他们展示了一个更大的饮料罐模型。
   >

43. He explained each part with slow hand movements.

   > 他用缓慢的手势解释了每个部分。
   >

44. The girl found the model easier to understand than the board.

   > 这个女孩发现这个模型比展示板更容易理解。
   >

45. Her friend chose the model as the main picture for their talk.

   > 她的朋友选择了这个模型作为他们演讲的主要图片。
   >

46. The expert made another claim about good design.

   > 专家又提出了一个关于好设计的说法。
   >

47. He said small changes gradually made the can less heavy and stronger.

   > 他说，小的改变逐渐让罐子变得更轻、更结实。
   >

48. The girl copied the sentence because it sounded useful.

   > 这个女孩抄下了这个句子，因为它听起来很有用。
   >

49. Her friend asked if they should include many numbers.

   > 她的朋友问他们是否应该包含很多数字。
   >

50. The expert answered that two or three numbers were enough.

   > 专家回答说，两三个数字就足够了。
   >

51. The girl felt less uncertainty after that answer.

   > 在那个回答之后，这个女孩感到更少的不确定。
   >

52. Her friend crossed out four extra facts from the page.

   > 她的朋友从纸上划掉了四条多余的事实。
   >

53. They kept the facts that helped their main idea.

   > 他们保留了帮助主要观点的事实。
   >

54. They avoided the facts that made the talk too heavy.

   > 他们避开了让演讲太沉重的事实。
   >

55. Before lunch, they sat in front of the library.

   > 午饭前，他们坐在图书馆前面。
   >

56. The girl explained the opening of the can to her friend.

   > 这个女孩向她的朋友解释了罐子的开口。
   >

57. Her friend counted the labels on their drawing.

   > 她的朋友数了他们图上的标签。
   >

58. They found six labels, so they stopped adding more.

   > 他们发现有六个标签，所以停止添加更多。
   >

59. The girl chose a clear title for the talk.

   > 这个女孩为演讲选择了一个清楚的标题。
   >

60. Her friend welcomed the title and wrote it at the top.

   > 她的朋友接受了这个标题，并把它写在顶部。
   >

61. In the afternoon, they went back to the teacher.

   > 下午，他们回到了老师那里。
   >

62. The teacher checked their plan and praised their clear notes from earlier.

   > 老师检查了他们的计划，并表扬了他们先前清楚的笔记。
   >

63. The teacher let the girl carry the empty can home in her bag.

   > 老师让这个女孩把空罐子装在包里带回家。
   >

64. Her friend went home with the drawing in his bag.

   > 她的朋友带着包里的图回了家。
   >

65. By evening, they had a simple but authentic plan for their talk.

   > 到了晚上，他们有了一个简单但真实的演讲计划。
   >
