## 正文

1. Today was a busy day in the garden.

   > 今天在花园里是个忙碌的日子。
   >

2. I started by emptying out the pots where the plants had grown too large.

   > 我首先把那些植物长得太大的盆清空。
   >

3. It felt like a real achievement to repot them into larger spaces.

   > 把它们重新移植到更大的地方感觉像个真正的成就。
   >

4. As I worked, the sweet scent of flowers surrounded me.

   > 工作时，花香围绕着我。
   >

5. There was a bench nearby where I could rest when I was tired.

   > 附近有张长椅，我累了可以休息。
   >

6. Taking care of the garden is definitely worth the effort.

   > 照顾花园绝对值得付出努力。
   >

7. My cat watched curiously from his favorite spot.

   > 我的猫从它最喜欢的位置好奇地观看。
   >

8. He often tries to play with the leaves, which can be frustrating.

   > 它经常试图玩树叶，这可能会令人沮丧。
   >

9. I needed a reminder to water the plants regularly.

   > 我需要一个提醒定期给植物浇水。
   >

10. Overwatering can be harmful to some plants.

    > 过度浇水对一些植物来说是有害的。
    >

11. I argued with myself about which plants needed more sunlight.

    > 我和自己争论哪些植物需要更多阳光。
    >

12. Using a small spoon, I carefully added fertilizer to the soil.

    > 用小勺子，我小心地在土壤中加入了肥料。
    >

13. I made sure to stir the soil gently.

    > 我确保轻轻地搅拌土壤。
    >

14. After that, I moved to the pet area.

    > 然后，我去了宠物区。
    >

15. Feeding the fish felt calming.

    > 喂鱼让人感到平静。
    >

16. I watched them swim in circles, their colors bright and vibrant.

    > 我看着它们在水中游来游去，色彩鲜艳。
    >

17. Cleaning the aquarium can be a waste of time if not done correctly.

    > 如果不正确地清理水族箱可能是浪费时间。
    >

18. I had to ensure the tank was not burned out by the warm lights.

    > 我必须确保鱼缸没有被暖灯烧坏。
    >

19. The empty pet beds needed fresh bedding.

    > 空的宠物床需要新的垫料。
    >

20. I found it relaxing to arrange everything nicely.

    > 把一切摆放得整整齐齐让我感到放松。
    >

21. My dog’s wagging tail was a clear sign of his happiness.

    > 我家狗摇晃的尾巴是它快乐的明显标志。
    >

22. Spending time with them was definitely a worthwhile activity.

    > 和它们共度时光绝对是值得的活动。
    >

23. Before finishing, I checked the gate to ensure it was secure.

    > 在结束前，我检查了大门以确保安全。
    >

24. This daily routine kept me engaged and active.

    > 这个日常工作让我忙碌又活跃。
    >

25. The sense of accomplishment after all the tasks were done was rewarding.

    > 完成所有任务后的成就感是很有回报的。
    >

26. As the sun set, everything was in place.

    > 太阳落山时，一切都井井有条。
    >

27. The garden and pets were taken care of for another day.

    > 花园和宠物又被照顾了一天。
    >

28. It was time to relax and enjoy the calm evening.

    > 该放松一下，享受宁静的夜晚了。
    >
