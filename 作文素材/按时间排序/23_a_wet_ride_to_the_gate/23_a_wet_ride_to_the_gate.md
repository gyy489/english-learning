# A Wet Ride to the Gate

- 天数：第 23 天
- 来源真题：无（纯生词复习）
- 来源文件：无（纯生词复习）
- 复习内容：D22 当前生词 + 到期旧词 + 积压清理（纯单词）

## 正文

1. Tom wanted to ride his bike to the park in the morning.

   > 汤姆早上想骑自行车去公园。
   >

2. He stood near the gate and checked his bag.

   > 他站在大门旁边，检查自己的包。
   >

3. His mother asked him to bring some bread for lunch.

   > 他的妈妈让他带一些面包当午饭。
   >

4. Tom put the bread in his bag and began to chew a small piece.

   > 汤姆把面包放进包里，并开始嚼一小块。
   >

5. The sky was gray, but he still moved forward.

   > 天空是灰色的，但他还是向前走。
   >

6. The road was wet because it rained last night.

   > 路是湿的，因为昨晚下过雨。
   >

7. Tom rode slowly through the quiet street.

   > 汤姆慢慢骑车穿过安静的街道。
   >

8. At first, the ride was smooth and easy.

   > 一开始，这次骑行很平稳，也很轻松。
   >

9. Suddenly, his bike made a strange sound.

   > 突然，他的自行车发出了奇怪的声音。
   >

10. Tom stopped and looked down at the chain.

   > 汤姆停下来，低头看链条。
   >

11. The chain was loose, and one wheel did not move well.

   > 链条松了，一个轮子也转得不太好。
   >

12. Tom tried to push the bike forward, but it was hard.

   > 汤姆试着把自行车向前推，但很困难。
   >

13. Then the front wheel hit a small stone.

   > 然后前轮撞到了一块小石头。
   >

14. Tom did not fall, but he felt worried.

   > 汤姆没有摔倒，但他感到担心。
   >

15. He walked his bike to a small repair shop.

   > 他推着自行车走到一家小修理店。
   >

16. A mechanic was standing outside the shop.

   > 一名修理工正站在店外。
   >

17. Tom asked the mechanic to repair the bike.

   > 汤姆请修理工修理自行车。
   >

18. The mechanic looked at the chain and the wheel.

   > 修理工看了看链条和轮子。
   >

19. He said the repair would not take long.

   > 他说修理不会花很长时间。
   >

20. Tom waited under a tree because the ground was still wet.

   > 汤姆在树下等着，因为地面仍然是湿的。
   >

21. After a few minutes, the mechanic called him back.

   > 几分钟后，修理工把他叫了回来。
   >

22. The bike was ready, and the wheel moved in a smooth way.

   > 自行车修好了，轮子转得很平稳。
   >

23. Tom thanked the mechanic and got on the bike again.

   > 汤姆感谢了修理工，又骑上了自行车。
   >

24. He rode through the street and soon saw the park appear ahead.

   > 他骑车穿过街道，很快看到公园出现在前方。
   >

25. When he reached the gate, he felt happy and calm.

   > 当他到达大门时，他感到开心又平静。
   >

26. He sat on a bench, opened his bag, and ate the bread slowly.

   > 他坐在长椅上，打开包，慢慢吃面包。
   >

27. This simple ride helped him remember to check his bike before leaving.

   > 这次简单的骑行让他记得出门前要检查自行车。
   >
